About Me

Hi! These pages are a short record of what I have been cooking. By day, I work as a software engineer at Cisco Systems in Bengaluru, on NGFWv (Next-Generation Virtual Firewalls). My professional interests lie in operating systems and networking, particularly how host software, virtualized devices, and network hardware work together for high-performance communication.


I am from Udupi, with family roots in Gokarna, India. I cook a lot of coastal food and usually prefer having people over for a soothing south-indian supper to going out.


PC games take up another corner of my free time. Sekiro, Elden Ring, and the Dark Souls series are favourites, alongside short, low-resolution indie horror games such as Fears to Fathom and Tales Beyond the Tomb. I would like to make some eery games myself someday.


Drawing has been part of my life for as long as I can remember, and eventually led to making short animations too. A few of my drawings and animations are collected here.
